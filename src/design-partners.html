DESIGN PARTNERS

Co-build governable AI for engineering teams

We are recruiting a small cohort of design partners — especially in fintech, platform engineering, and regulated digital teams — to pilot ContextMint before general availability. Intake reopens by end of July 2026 while we finish v1.0 pilot readiness. The program outline below is for reference; the form is paused until then.

The 30-day evaluation pilot

A design partner pilot is a bounded one-month proof-of-concept — not a subscription that ends after 30 days, and not a requirement for a 30-minute sales call to start (async via the form is fine).

What you commit
  • Named pilot lead (platform, security, or engineering)
  • 10–30 developers on one team or repo
  • 30 calendar days of real dev usage after onboarding
  • Short feedback at week 2 and week 4 (async OK)
What we deliver
  • Install help + deployment path review (local or on-prem)
  • Weekly async check-in during the pilot month
  • Written pilot summary for security and platform stakeholders
  • Option to extend to 90 days if the pilot is going well

What you get

  • Early access to ContextMint Engine + VS Code extension
  • Local-first deployment — or shared on-prem server with guided path review
  • Context Lens + quality map for audit-friendly AI-assisted development
  • Direct line to product engineering; influence the roadmap
  • Pilot summary report for your security and platform stakeholders

What we ask

  • Honest assessment of fit for regulated / sovereign AI dev workflows
  • If Track B matters: which systems to connect first (Jira, Confluence, SharePoint, etc.)
  • Willingness to share blocker-level feedback — not a marketing testimonial

What we are building next

Design partners shape three horizons — not just today’s pilot. See the full product roadmap.

v1.0 Decision-control plane

Engine, Context Lens, quality map, symbol-aware retrieval — shipping end of August 2026.

v2.0 · Q1 2027 Engineering Intelligence Platform

Knowledge Fabric, institutional memory, CHRONOS forensics, operational intelligence retrieval — one digital twin of your engineering system.

Track B · end 2027 Enterprise & compliance

Jira, Confluence, SharePoint, Git, and org knowledge bases — plus SSO/RBAC, multi-tenant isolation, tamper-evident audit, and SOC 2 evidence packaging.

Especially interested in v2.0 + Track B (Jira, Confluence, SharePoint, Git, and other knowledge bases)? Note that in the form when intake opens — we prioritize partners who need unified org knowledge and compliance evidence, not just IDE chat.

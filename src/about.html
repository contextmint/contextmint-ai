OUR MISSION

Evidence before AI ships

ContextMint is a local-first AI decision-control plane for software teams — so engineers see evidence before inference and approve changes before they apply.

The problem we solve

AI can produce changes faster than organizations can review them with evidence. Knowledge is fragmented across git, CI, docs, and ticketing. Reviewers merge with incomplete context.

ContextMint indexes the workspace, surfaces stored governance findings, and puts human gates in front of inference and repo writes — on your infrastructure by default.

How we are different

  • Evidence first — Context Lens and citations before the model answers
  • Local-first — code stays on your machine; privacy by architecture
  • Human apply gates — patch preview; no silent auto-apply
  • Agent-native (roadmap) — planned MCP tools; v1.0 uses IDE Gates 1 & 2

Principles

Truth over speed

Human gates before inference and before apply.

Evidence over guesses

Indexed excerpts and stored findings beat ungrounded chat.

Governance by design

Quality map, apply gates, and operator audit trails in v1.0.

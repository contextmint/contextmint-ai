OUR MISSION

Weave intelligence into your codebase

ContextMint reconstructs engineering reality — ownership, dependencies, architecture intent, and policy — so teams trust what ships, especially in the age of AI-generated code.

The problem we solve

AI can produce pull requests faster than organizations can verify them. Knowledge is fragmented across git, CI, docs, and ticketing. Reviewers merge with incomplete context.

ContextMint builds a continuously updated Engineering Reality Graph — deterministic, evidence-backed, and queryable — so every change is evaluated against what your organization actually knows.

How we are different

  • Deterministic first — graph truth is authoritative; AI narrates, never overrides
  • Local-first — code stays on your machine; privacy by architecture
  • Evidence on every claim — CODEOWNERS, ADRs, policies, dependency paths cited
  • Agent-native (roadmap) — planned MCP read tools and RBAC for AI assistants; v1.0 uses human Gates 1 & 2 in the IDE

Principles

Truth over speed

Verification gates before auto-apply. Humans confirm material changes.

Graph over grep

Structured relationships beat keyword search for impact and ownership.

Governance by design

Audit trails, RBAC, and policy graphs for enterprise readiness.

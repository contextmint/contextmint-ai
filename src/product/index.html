Under the hood (vision)

The Engineering Reality Graph direction reconstructs ownership, dependencies, architecture intent, deployment context, and policy into one queryable source of truth. Deterministic first; AI narrates with citations — never overrides graph truth without human confirmation. v1.0 ships indexed workspace evidence, Findings Store lookup, and IDE gates — not the full graph product below.

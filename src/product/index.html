Under the hood

The Engineering Reality Graph reconstructs ownership, dependencies, architecture intent, deployment context, and policy into one queryable source of truth. Deterministic first; AI narrates with fabric citations — never overrides graph truth without human confirmation.

Engineering Reality Graph
├── ownership graph
├── dependency / blast radius graph
├── architecture intent (ADRs, decisions)
├── policy graph (PCI, RBAC, custom rules)
└── drift baselines (accepted vs violation)

Deterministic verification

  • Drift — graph vs contract/convention
  • DNA report — deterministic body

AI-assisted, not AI-decided

Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

Overview

ContextMint Ltd (“ContextMint”, “we”) provides engineering intelligence software. This policy describes how we collect, use, and protect information when you use our website, VS Code extension (ContextLoom), and related services.

Local-first tier

On the Developer tier, repository indexing, embeddings, and chat inference run locally on your machine. We do not receive your source code unless you explicitly enable cloud features or contact support with diagnostic logs you choose to share.

Information we collect

  • Account data — name, email, company when you register or request a demo
  • Usage telemetry — optional, anonymized product analytics on Team and Enterprise tiers
  • Website data — standard server logs and cookies for site functionality
  • Billing — payment information processed by Stripe; we do not store full card numbers

How we use information

  • Provide, maintain, and improve the ContextMint platform
  • Respond to demo requests and support inquiries
  • Process subscriptions and send billing-related communications
  • Comply with legal obligations

Data retention

Account data is retained while your subscription is active and for a reasonable period afterward for legal and billing purposes. You may request deletion by contacting privacy@contextmint.ai.

Your rights

Depending on your jurisdiction (including UK GDPR), you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or export your personal data. Contact us to exercise these rights.
